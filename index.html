The problem

Renewal prep, certificate requests, quote follow-up, and dual entry between systems eat owner and staff time—while leads and service requests wait. The cost shows up as slower response, dropped handoffs, and hours that never reach production or growth.

What we do

Diagnose first. Implement with a fixed scope.

Cassian is a diagnostic and implementation partner—not an open-ended custom build shop. We find the workflows creating avoidable friction, then configure and launch the most appropriate prepackaged automation system with a clear timeline and success measure.

  1. 01

    Introductory call

    A free 15-minute fit conversation. We explore which processes are manual, delayed, or dropped—and whether a productized system is a credible next step.

  2. 02

    Paid workflow diagnostic

    Structured interviews and analysis that quantify pain, prioritize opportunities, and recommend a specific system—with a written report useful even if you do not proceed to implementation.

  3. 03

    Implementation & launch

    Fixed-scope setup: configure tools, connect approved systems, define human review rules, train the team, and go live against a measurement plan.

  4. 04

    Optimization retainer

    Ongoing monitoring, refinements, and adoption support—without promising unlimited custom development.

About

Built for operators, not demos.

Cassian AI was founded by Jahleel Jackson, an AI engineer who has shipped systems for a Fortune 500 consulting firm, a federally funded research institute, and an NSF-backed product incubator.

The pattern across every engagement was the same: leadership knew automation could help, but no one had turned that intuition into a concrete plan tied to real workflows and measurable return.

We work with owner-operated independent insurance agencies that want technology to compound their advantage—without compromising the craft and relationships that built them.
